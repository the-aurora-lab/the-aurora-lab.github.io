Reviewer(s)' Comments to Author:

Reviewer: 1

Comments to the Author
The authors map urban heat with sensors on shared bicycles. The idea is good and the paper is short and clear.

1. How were the sensors calibrated? Twelve sensors can drift apart; please describe an intercomparison.
2. Readings taken while a bicycle is parked in the sun are likely biased. How were they handled?
3. Figure 1 would be easier to read with the bicycles' routes shown.

Reviewer: 2

Comments to the Author
This is a useful demonstration of low-cost mobile sensing.

1. One summer in one city is a narrow basis for the conclusions. Please discuss how general the result is.
2. Why bicycles rather than cars? A sentence on the advantages would help.
3. Please give the uncertainty of the 1.4 degree difference.
